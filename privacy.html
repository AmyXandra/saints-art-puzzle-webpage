Privacy policy

This policy explains what information Art of Devotion Games collects when you play our games or visit our websites, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We collect as little as we can. We never sell your information.

01Who we are

Art of Devotion Games ("we", "us") makes Saints Art Puzzle. You can reach us at artofdevotiongames@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Saints Art Puzzle app on iPhone and Android, the web game at saintsartpuzzle.com (including when it is embedded on other websites), and artofdevotiongames.com.

02What we collect

Information you give us

  • A display name, if you choose to join the daily league. It is shown on the leaderboard, where other players can see it. We ask you not to use your real name, and we filter names for personal details.
  • Your chosen face (player picture). This stays on your device.
  • Messages you send us, such as a support request, and the email address you send them from.

Game information

  • Your progress: saints completed, stories, coins, streaks and purchases.
  • League scores and puzzle finish times. Our server checks these so nobody can cheat.

Device and advertising information

  • Device type, operating system, app version, crash reports, and your country from your phone's region setting (never your precise location).
  • An advertising identifier, used by our ad partners to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first.

Purchases

  • Any in-app purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We receive a confirmation of the purchase, never your card details.

On our websites

  • The web game saves your streak and whether you have played today in your browser's storage. It stays on your computer or phone.
  • Ads on saintsartpuzzle.com are provided by Google AdSense and may use cookies. Where required, we ask for your consent with a cookie banner.

What we do not collect

  • Your birthday, real name, precise location, contacts, photos or microphone. There is no chat in our games.

03How we use it

  • To run the game: save progress, place you in a league, and show leaderboards.
  • To keep things fair: check scores and stop cheating.
  • To show ads.
  • To answer your messages and fix problems.
  • To understand, in total, how the game is used so we can improve it.
  • To keep our games and services secure, and to meet our legal obligations.

04Ads

Our games are free and supported by ads. In the app, ads come from Unity Ads and Google AdMob. On saintsartpuzzle.com, ads come from Google AdSense. We block ads about gambling, dating, alcohol and fashion. Watching a reward video is always your choice.

When a player may be a child, we ask our ad partners to show only non-personalised ads. You can limit ad tracking or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

On saintsartpuzzle.com, Google and its partners use cookies to show and measure ads. Because the site is made for families, we have asked Google to treat it as directed at children, so the ads are not based on your past visits to this or other websites. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and manage your Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

05Who we share it with

We share information only with companies that help us run the games, and only what they need:

  • Google Firebase (hosting, saving progress, and Firebase Analytics)
  • Unity Ads and Google AdMob (ads in the app)
  • Google AdSense (ads on saintsartpuzzle.com)
  • Apple and Google (app stores and purchases)

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect players or our legal rights, with your consent, or as part of a merger or sale of the company. We never sell personal information.

06Children

Saints Art Puzzle is made for families, and children play it. We follow Apple's and Google's rules for family apps and laws such as COPPA (USA) and GDPR (Europe and UK).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what the game needs to work. A parent or guardian can ask us to see or delete a child's information at any time by writing to artofdevotiongames@gmail.com.

07Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data by emailing artofdevotiongames@gmail.com. We delete it within 30 days.
  • Limit ads through your phone's advertising settings.
  • Clear the web game by clearing your browser's site data.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California or Nigeria), you may have the right to see, correct, delete or move your information, to object to or limit how we use it, and to withdraw consent you have given. Write to us and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

08Other services

Our games link to the App Store and Google Play, and our websites may link to other sites. They have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your information.

09How long we keep it

  • Progress and league data: until you ask us to delete it.
  • League results: 90 days, then only totals are kept.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.

10Keeping it safe

We use encrypted connections and the security built into our providers. No system is perfect, so please tell us at once if you think something is wrong.

11International transfers

Our providers may store information in other countries, such as the United States. When they do, we rely on safeguards the law allows, such as standard contractual clauses.

12Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the change is important, we will tell you in the app.
