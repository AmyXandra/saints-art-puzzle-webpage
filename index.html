HOW TO PLAY

Three steps, about three minutes

  1. 01

    Pick a piece

    Each piece is a part of the painting: a figure, a cloud, a golden gate.

  2. 02

    Drop it in place

    Drag it onto the line drawing. A gold glow shows where it fits when you use a hint.

  3. 03

    Reveal the saint

    Place the last piece to reveal who it is, then read their story and a prayer.

FAQ

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes. The website and the app are both free, with a few ads.

Is it safe for children?

Yes. There is no chat, no violence, and we block ads for gambling, dating and alcohol.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, on phones, tablets and computers. For the best experience, try fullscreen.

Can I put it on my parish website?

Yes. Copy this code and paste it into any page. Change your-custom-src to a short name for your parish or website.

<iframe src="https://play.saintsartpuzzle.com/?partner=your-custom-src" title="Saints Art Puzzle" style="width: 100%; max-width: 846px; height: 540px; border: 0;" allow="fullscreen; autoplay" allowfullscreen></iframe>
Is my progress saved?

Your streak is saved in this browser, and the app saves your progress on your phone. Saving across devices is coming soon.
