HOW TO PLAY

Three steps, about three minutes

01
Pick a piece

Each piece is a part of the painting: a figure, a cloud, a golden gate.

Drag it onto the line drawing. A gold glow shows where it fits when you use a hint.

FAQ

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes. The website is free with a few ads. The app is free too, with an optional purchase to remove ads.

Is it safe for children?

Yes. There is no chat, no violence, and we block ads for gambling, dating and alcohol.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, on phones, tablets and computers. For the best experience, try fullscreen.

Can I put it on my parish website?

Yes. Copy the free embed code and paste it into any page.

Is my progress saved?

Your streak is saved in this browser. To keep everything across devices, use the app.
